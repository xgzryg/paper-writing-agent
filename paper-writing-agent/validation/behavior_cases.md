## Case 08

用户消息：

> 下面是我想压缩的Discussion段落，目标大约减半。先给具体精简建议并告诉我哪些内容会被删，我选定后你再改正文。保留数字和参考文献，不要自行把限制删掉，也不要联网。
>
> Our study found an association between irregular sleep timing and lower patient-reported recovery scores 30 days after surgery. The association remained after adjustment for age and procedure type. These findings suggest that sleep regularity may be relevant to postoperative recovery, although the observational design does not establish causality. Previous studies have linked sleep disruption to several aspects of physical recovery [12,13]. In the present cohort, 186 adults contributed complete outcome data, and the adjusted difference in recovery score was 4.2 points (95% CI, 1.1–7.3). The single-centre design may limit generalisability. Sleep was measured for seven days before surgery, so longer-term patterns were not captured. Future studies should evaluate whether changes in sleep regularity improve recovery.
