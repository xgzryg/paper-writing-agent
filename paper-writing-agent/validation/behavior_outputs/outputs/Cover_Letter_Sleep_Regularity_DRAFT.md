Among 186 adults undergoing elective surgery, greater preoperative sleep regularity was associated with higher patient-reported recovery scores at 30 days after adjustment for age and procedure type. By examining sleep regularity in relation to patients' postoperative recovery, this observational study provides evidence to inform further investigation of sleep patterns in surgical care and is relevant to the journal's stated focus on clinical perioperative care and postoperative rehabilitation.
